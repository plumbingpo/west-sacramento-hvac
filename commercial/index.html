Restaurant Services

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and an undersized or failing system shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines dump enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

A kitchen stuck in negative pressure is miserable to work in, and customers notice the drafts at the door and the stuffy dining room right away. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases require.

Your schedule comes first. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. West Sacramento HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.
