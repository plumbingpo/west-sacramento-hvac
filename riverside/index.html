The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or gives off a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can prevent a much larger repair.

Keeping Your Riverside HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Small problems rarely stay small for long. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a miserable night.
